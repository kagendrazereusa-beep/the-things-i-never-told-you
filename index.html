01 — The Letters

in the quietest
part of me.

A letter for the things that were easier to write than to say out loud.

An unsent letter · For Ran

Ran,

Aku akhir-akhir ini sering berpikir tentang betapa anehnya merindukan seseorang tanpa benar-benar tahu bagian mana dari dirinya yang paling kita rindukan.

Mungkin aku merindukan percakapan kita. Cara hal-hal kecil bisa menjadi cerita panjang ketika aku berbicara denganmu. Atau mungkin aku hanya merindukan perasaan bahwa, untuk beberapa saat, ada seseorang yang membuat hari-hariku terasa sedikit lebih ringan.

I miss you, but not in a way that asks you to come back and make everything the way it used to be. I don't think I want to turn back time. There are things that have changed, and perhaps some things are meant to stay changed.

Aku hanya kadang bertanya-tanya apakah kamu juga mengingatnya. Apakah ada hal kecil yang tiba-tiba membuatmu teringat kepadaku, atau apakah aku hanya menyimpan begitu banyak kenangan yang ternyata tidak pernah tinggal lama di sisimu.

I hope life has been kind to you, Ran. I hope you have reasons to laugh on ordinary days, people who listen when you need to talk, and somewhere you can return to when the world feels a little too loud.

And if I am no longer the person you turn to, I hope you know that I still wish you well. Maybe that is one of the quieter ways of loving someone: allowing them to find happiness, even when you are no longer at the center of it.

I'm glad it was you, Ran.

I don't know where this feeling will take me. For now, I suppose I can let it exist without asking it to become anything else. Some feelings don't need an answer to have been real.

With a little more honesty than usual, and a warmth I don't always know how to show,

Jean
